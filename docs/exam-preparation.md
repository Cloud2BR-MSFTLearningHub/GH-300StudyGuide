# Study plan and exam readiness

**Start here:** take a closed-notes diagnostic, then spend time on weak skills
rather than rereading every chapter equally.

This guide contains **88 original study questions**: 48 foundation/path-review
questions plus a 40-question weighted mock. They are not actual exam questions,
a dump, or a prediction of your exam result.

## Verify official logistics

As checked on November 22, 2026, the
[certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/github-copilot/)
lists **100 minutes** for the assessment and links to the practice assessment,
exam experience demo, scheduling, and applicable policies.
Confirm your booked exam's language, provider instructions, time, permitted
resources, identification, accommodations, and equipment requirements directly
with Microsoft/Pearson VUE. Do not assume this study site's timing or navigation
matches the real exam.

The study guide links to a passing scaled score of **700**.
That is **not** a promise that 70% on these questions passes the exam; scaled
exam scores and this guide's simple practice percentage are different.
Use the official [scoring explanation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exam-scoring-reports).

## Diagnostic and error log

1. Answer [M01-M40](mock-exam.md) without opening explanations.
2. Mark each answer **confident**, **uncertain**, or **guessed**.
3. Score by domain; count lucky guesses as topics needing review.
4. Record the decision you missed, the correct rule, why your choice failed,
   an official source, and a small verification exercise.
5. Revisit after a day, then after several days with a changed scenario.

Copy this non-sensitive template into your own study notes:

| Question/skill | My mistaken assumption | Correct rule and source | Evidence/exercise | Revisit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example: instruction versus policy | A Markdown instruction blocks tools | Policies and permissions enforce access; instructions guide behavior | Explain a denied-tool scenario | Tomorrow |

Do not store work credentials, private source code, real audit records, or
personal exam details in a public study repository.

this is a planning recommendation, not an exam requirement.

If time is short, prioritize features (the largest domain), your worst
diagnostic domains, and the hands-on tests. Do not skip privacy or responsible
use simply because code generation feels familiar.

## Readiness is evidence, not a promise

A useful **personal study target** is at least 80% overall, no domain below
80%, and the ability to justify every selected and rejected option.
These thresholds are deliberately conservative self-checks, not official
passing criteria or a statistical prediction.

- [ ] I can explain the six domains without confusing features with governance.
- [ ] I completed all six labs or documented an entitlement-related alternative.
- [ ] I can show a test failing on a wrong implementation and passing after repair.
- [ ] I can distinguish client, session, environment, context, and permissions.
- [ ] I know the current limits of exclusions and public-code filters.
- [ ] I used the official practice assessment and reviewed explanations.
- [ ] I can answer new examples, not just recognize memorized answer letters.
- [ ] I checked the official objectives and booked-exam instructions again.

Retaking the same bank measures recall as well as understanding.
Change the scenario, teach the rule aloud, and use official assessments
to avoid false confidence.

## During practice and the exam

Identify the actor, client/environment, desired result, permission boundary,
and whether the question asks for the **first**, **best**, or **two** actions.
Avoid inventing facts absent from the scenario.
For multi-select items, honor the stated selection count.
Use only navigation and review options the actual exam interface permits;
some sections may have different rules.

Use the official [exam experience demo](https://GHCertDemo.starttest.com)
before the appointment. Do not seek or redistribute recalled exam questions.
